We work with communities to respond to practical needs and build pathways to long-term wellbeing. Our work is organised around four connected pillars.

<a id="economic"></a>
## 01 — Economic empowerment

We support income-generating activities and financial literacy for women and young people. Economic empowerment helps households strengthen livelihoods, build resilience and make informed financial decisions.

![Community economic empowerment activities](/program/empowerment.jpg)

![Community distribution activity](/program/distribution.jpg)

<a id="education"></a>
## 02 — School mentorship & education

We promote access to education, life skills and mentorship for vulnerable students. We believe that learning and guidance can help children and young people realise their potential and participate fully in community life.

![Students participating in an education activity](/program/students_in_field.jpg)

![Mentorship activity](/program/mentorship.jpg)

<a id="menstrual"></a>
## 03 — Menstrual hygiene awareness

We work to break stigma through menstrual health education and access to menstrual products, supporting dignity, confidence and participation in education and everyday life.

![Menstrual health programme](/program/menstrual_health.jpg)

![Menstrual products distribution](/program/pads.jpg)

<a id="housing"></a>
## 04 — Decent housing & sanitation

We seek to enhance living conditions through improved shelter and hygiene infrastructure, contributing to safer and healthier households.

![Housing improvement activity](/program/housing.jpg)

![Housing and shelter](/program/hut.jpg)
