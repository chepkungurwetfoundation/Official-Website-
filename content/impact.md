## Creating tangible change

Chepkungurwet Foundation is dedicated to the holistic transformation of rural families and communities through sustainable development initiatives.

Our programs focus on the interconnected challenges that shape everyday life: household livelihoods, access to education, menstrual health, decent shelter and sanitation.

## Transforming lives through our programs

### Economic empowerment
Supporting income-generating activities and financial literacy for women and young people.

### Education & mentorship
Promoting access to education, life skills and mentorship for vulnerable students.

### Menstrual health
Breaking stigma through menstrual health education and access to menstrual products.

### Housing & sanitation
Enhancing living conditions through improved shelter and hygiene infrastructure.

## Our goals for the future

We want rural communities to have the resources and knowledge to thrive sustainably. We pursue this through community-led development that empowers local residents to be agents of their own transformation.
