## Who we are

Chepkungurwet Foundation is a grassroots organisation committed to empowering women, children and families across Kenya. We focus on holistic, community-driven solutions that uplift lives and restore dignity.

We are dedicated to the holistic transformation of rural families and communities through sustainable development initiatives that address economic, social and environmental challenges.

## Our vision

A world where every rural community has the resources and knowledge to thrive sustainably.

## Our approach

We believe that community members should be agents of their own transformation. Our work is grounded in locally led development, practical support and collaboration.
